About LakbayAral

Empowering the Next Generation of Tourism Leaders

LakbayAral (from the Filipino words "Lakbay" meaning travel and "Aral" meaning learning) is an e-learning platform built specifically for Senior High School students under the TVL-Home Economics Tourism strand in the Philippines.

We bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry readiness by providing interactive, DepEd-aligned courses that prepare students for TESDA National Certificates and careers in the Philippine tourism industry.

✅

K-12 Curriculum Aligned

All content follows the DepEd prescribed curriculum guide for Tourism.

🌱

100% Filipino Content

Case studies, examples, and scenarios all set in the Philippine context.

🚀

Career-Ready Skills

Practical skills employers in tourism and hospitality look for.
