About LakbayAral

Empowering the Next Generation of Tourism Professionals

LakbayAral is a dedicated e-learning platform built for Senior High School students under the TVL-Tourism track aligned with the Philippine DepEd K-12 curriculum. We bridge classroom learning with real-world tourism competencies.

DepEd K-12 Aligned

All modules follow the prescribed curriculum for TVL-Tourism Promotion Services.

TESDA Competency-Based

Prepares you for National Certificate assessments in Tourism.

Interactive & Engaging

Video lessons, virtual tours, quizzes, and hands-on activities.
